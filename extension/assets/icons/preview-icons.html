📋 Next Steps

  1. Choose your favorite design from the options above
  2. Convert SVG to PNG at the required sizes (16×16, 48×48, 128×128)
  3. Replace the existing icons in extension/assets/icons/
  4. Update manifest.json to reference the new icons

How to convert SVG to PNG:

  • Online: Use CloudConvert or ConvertIO
  • Command line: Use ImageMagick: magick -density 300 icon.svg -resize 128x128 icon-128.png
  • Design tools: Open in Figma, Sketch, or Adobe Illustrator and export as PNG
