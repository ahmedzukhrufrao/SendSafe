# Privacy Policy for SendSafe

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Introduction

SendSafe ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SendSafe Chrome extension (the "Extension"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Extension.

By using SendSafe, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

When you paste content into a Gmail compose window, SendSafe collects the pasted text content to analyze it for AI-generated traces. We may also collect your IP address temporarily for service operation purposes.

## How We Use Your Information

The pasted email text is used solely to analyze whether the content contains AI-generated artifacts and to display the results to you. Your pasted text is sent to a third-party AI analysis service for processing and is not stored on our servers after analysis is complete.

## Data Storage

We do not store your pasted email content. The text is processed and discarded immediately after analysis. We do not retain copies of your email content.

## Third-Party Services

SendSafe uses third-party AI analysis services to analyze pasted text. Your pasted text is sent to these services for analysis according to their respective privacy policies. We have configured our service to opt out of data training, meaning your content is not used to train AI models.

## Data Security

We use HTTPS encryption to protect data transmission. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information or email content to third parties. We share pasted text with third-party AI analysis services solely for the purpose of AI trace detection. We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## Your Rights and Choices

You can disable or uninstall SendSafe at any time through Chrome's extension management page (`chrome://extensions/`). Since we do not store your pasted email content, there is no stored data to access or delete.

## Children's Privacy

SendSafe is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. By using SendSafe, you consent to these transfers.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

## Chrome Web Store Permissions

SendSafe requests access to `https://mail.google.com/*` to detect paste events in Gmail compose windows. SendSafe does not request access to your browsing history, other websites, storage, camera, microphone, or location data.
